How to read a listed company from its own filings: the three financial
statements, how well it earns, how much it borrows, whether its profit turns
into cash, what the market charges for it, and how a discounted cash flow
(DCF) valuation is built.

Start from a question, or read the explainers in order. Most work an example
on one fictional company's books, so every ratio uses the same numbers. No
stock is recommended.
