## Method

the **rendered** output — every `_data/` value filled in — against `CLAUDE.md`

sourcing, audience layering). Worked examples were recomputed, TA Python
snippets re-run against `assets/data/`, and the key numeric P0s re-checked
independently before fixing.

## What the P0s were (all fixed)

Grouped by kind of mistake — the useful part for future writing.

- **A claim reversed or contradicted by its own data.** SIP vs lump sum from
  2007 (the lump sum actually won); the drawdown table promising "every fall
  over 15%" but missing three (one still unrecovered); STP/SWP "three
  different winners" (two); relative strength "spent the first eight months
  falling" (it rose first); EBITDA-margin compression "mostly carries through"
  (less than half did); volatility clustering described backwards; SWP
  withdrawals "capital first, gains later" (FIFO makes it the reverse);
  capitalising expenses "inflates profit without cash" (it raises operating
  cash flow).
- **Unlike things compared.** Pre-tax ROCE vs post-tax ROE, used to conclude
  leverage wasn't inflating ROE; a P/B gap attributed to ROE when it was mostly
  IPO cash sitting in book value; a contingent-liability percentage computed on
  ₹145 lakh but described as the ₹120 lakh demand.
- **Wrong terms.** "Undiluted/diluted EPS" for pre-/post-IPO share counts
  (Ind AS 33 means something else) — across four posts; a "52% ROE earns more
  than book every year".
- **Arithmetic and ranges.** Earnings yield "3.73% at P/E 38" (2.63%); "one
  percentage point" sensitivity ranges that were two; a diversification
  takeaway quoting the infinite-fund floor as the ten-fund figure; the HDFC Bank
  balance sheet not summing (two lines missing); a half-year amortisation
  charged for a full year.
- **Logic.** "90% of repeat losers lost again → not luck" when 88% of everyone
  lost; option *buyers* given the option-seller payoff; "XIRR doesn't depend
  on order".
- **Stale or wrong rules.** Debt-fund indexation still shown for old units
  (gone since 23 Jul 2024); gold ETFs still slab-rate (Finance Act 2025
  redefined "specified mutual fund"); a same-year short-term loss "carried
  forward" instead of set off; the credit-card risk weight (150% since
  Nov 2023); SEBI's category-rebalancing window (one month); the ESOP
  fair-market-value rule (open/close average).
- **Rendering and data bugs.** A line starting with a number + ". " turned
  sentences into list items (four posts, two already live); hard-coded prose
  numbers that had drifted (₹5,289; "20-day"; "nearly eight months"); a
  Bollinger share computed with missing values in the denominator; a string in
  a percentage field.

## Themes behind the P1s and P2s (all fixed)

- Soft verdicts on real listed companies ("not a mispricing", "rich but not
  irrational", "honest of them"), and justified-P/B scenarios run on a real
  bank.
- Acronyms not defined in every post, and mandatory jargon links missing.
- Series-structure copy gone stale ("the next nine posts", "closes the series").
- Hindsight endpoints in TA outcomes; RSI warm-up and seeding.
- Unsourced research and market claims.
- Desi Bites described as an SME listing while the posts applied main-board
  rules — resolved by making it main-board.
- Tax: surcharge never mentioned, cess applied unevenly, the example fund
  never named.
- Formatting: ".0" leaks, hyphen-minus and double negatives, ISO dates in
  sentences, title case in running text, takeaways too long to tweet.

## What changed as a result

- `CLAUDE.md` gained "Accuracy rules", "Rules, regulations and tax", the Desi
  Bites fixed points, the build pitfalls and the style rules above — the
  checklist for every new post.
- Shared includes now handle Indian grouping, true minus signs and all-zero
  decimals in tables and prose.
- Data scripts gained invariant checks (balance sheets must sum; shares
  computed on non-missing data).
- `fno.yml` was checked against SEBI's own study PDFs; the tax series was
  re-checked against primary sources where reachable.
