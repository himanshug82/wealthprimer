CLAUDE.md and an editorial brief (accuracy, clarity, tone, structure, takeaways,

(every `_data/` value filled in) and worked examples were recomputed; TA
snippets were re-run against `assets/data/`. Key numeric P0s were then
spot-checked independently (marked ✔ below).

**What's genuinely strong, everywhere:** no buy/sell calls or hype words anywhere;
the disclaimer is on all 116 posts; every worked example uses data well past the
3-month lag with the as-of date stated; the Desi Bites case study reconciles
across all statements; nearly all arithmetic recomputes exactly; and the honest

are the brand working as intended. The problems are mostly *interpretation*
sentences that say more than the numbers do, a handful of stale rules, and copy
that went stale as series grew.

## Cross-cutting themes (fix once, fix many)

1. **List-render bug class.** A line that starts with a number + ". " (a Liquid
   value or a year) turns a sentence into a list item. Live in 4 posts (above).
   Worth a tiny pre-build check in `scripts/` that flags `^(\d+|\{\{.*\}\})\.\s`
   after a non-blank, non-list line.
2. **Hard-coded numbers in prose drift from the data** — 5,289; "20-day";
   "first eight months"; "nearly eight months"; "Fifty-nine posts". Pull from
   Liquid or remove.
3. **Data-script bugs** — `derive_ta2.py` (NaN denominators, 0-dp rounding printed
   as ".0"), `build_case_study_2.py` (half-year amortisation), `derive_bank.py`
   (balance sheet doesn't sum), `jargon_m6.yml` (string in a % field). The RS and
   multi-timeframe Python snippets print numbers that don't match their own
   tables (warm-up NaNs counted as False).
4. **Stale series-structure copy** as series grew: TA posts 1 & 10 describe a
   10-post arc; Tax parts 1–9 describe 9 parts ("didn't cover … derivatives",
   now Part 12); MF factsheet post "closes the series"; PEG "closing note for
   this whole series"; Capstone is "Module 1 capstone".
5. **Commentary edging toward a verdict on a real stock** (P1, compliance tone):
   Britannia "rich but not irrational", "not a mispricing", "not valued cheaply
   vs peers", "which is honest of them", Capstone defending P/B/PEG; HDFC
   justified-P/B range "comfortably brackets" the actual — contradicts the WACC
   post's own rule of not publishing a discount rate for a real company. Keep
   real-company sections to mechanics.
6. **Pre-tax vs post-tax comparisons** — ROCE vs ROE (P0 above), ROCE vs WACC in
   the WACC and capital-allocation posts.
7. **Mandatory jargon links / undefined acronyms** — widespread: PAT, OCF, EBIT,
   CAGR, SIP, NAV, AMC, AMFI, TER, AUM, YTM, G-Sec, ELSS, PBT, EMI, GST, IPO, SME,
   FIFO, RSI… CLAUDE.md requires an inline definition in *every* post and a link
   whenever a post exists. Also two placeholder links to `/` (balance-sheet and
   cash-flow posts → inventory-days / free-cash-flow).
8. **Desi Bites is an NSE Emerge (SME) listing but FA module 2 applies
   main-board rules** — SME firms report half-yearly (no Q1/Q2); ICDR GCP cap and
   lock-ins differ; the fictional objects (40.6% GCP + unidentified acquisition)
   breach even the main-board 35% cap; the DRHP is dated March 2025 but holds
   FY25 restated accounts. Simplest fix: make Desi Bites main-board.
9. **Tax series**: surcharge never mentioned; cess applied inconsistently; the
   "real fund" is never named or sourced; TODO.md's "professional review still
   open" applies to every post.
10. **Hindsight endpoints in TA** — RSI outcomes measured to the eventual peak/
    trough; use a fixed horizon as the Bollinger post does.
11. **Unsourced research/market claims** — "acquisitions destroy value" as one of
    the most robust findings, simple TA rules "modest at best", flows "reliably"
    peak at highs, "most common error", "the ratio agencies reach for first".
    Cite or frame as opinion.
12. **Formatting** — ".0" leakage (₹5,851.0, 60.0%), hyphen-minus vs "−",
    double negatives ("fell -59.7%"), ISO dates in prose, title-case terms in
    Module 5 H1s, "Rs"/capital-L "Lakh" in the DRHP post, a duplicated
    "daily, Daily bars" chart caption across ~8 TA posts.
13. **Takeaways too long to tweet** — most run 60–90 words; CLAUDE.md wants 2–3
    standalone sentences. Lead each with a ≤280-character line.
14. **Known VERIFY items still open in TODO.md** — `fno.yml` vs SEBI's Aug 2026
    PDFs (FY25 loss ₹1.12 L cr may conflict with SEBI's July 2025 ~₹1.06 L cr;
    arithmetic points to 78.6 lakh FY26 traders); SEBI TER slabs (possibly
    superseded by the 2025 MF-regulation rewrite); category thresholds; KAM date;
    CASA FY24; D-SIB bucket (HDFC likely 0.40% from Apr 2025); overnight-fund
    mandate date; TA Module-2 SVGs not eyeballed; Google Sheet calculators owed.
