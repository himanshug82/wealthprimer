# SEO / Search Console audit — 25 October 2026

Scope: the live site (wealthprimer.in) plus a full local build of all 168 posts and 13
other pages, read the way Googlebot sees them: `<head>`, structured data, indexability,
sitemap, internal links, images, headings. This folder starts with `_`, so Jekyll never
publishes it.

## Verdict

The foundations were already better than most finance blogs: HTTPS with an
http→https redirect, a clean `robots.txt` pointing at the sitemap, correct canonicals on
every page, unique titles and descriptions, `BlogPosting` JSON-LD and a unique OG card on
every post, no broken internal links, no orphan posts, a median of 5 inbound internal
links per post, descriptive anchor text (2 generic anchors in 1,125 links), one `<h1>` per
post and no skipped heading levels.

Six structural problems were holding it back. All six are fixed in this change; four
things only the owner can do are listed at the end.

## Fixed in this change

| # | Problem (as found) | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **156 of 168 post titles over 60 characters** in the `<title>` tag (median 76) — jekyll-seo-tag appends " \| Wealth Primer" to every title. | Google truncates at about 60 characters, so the keyword-bearing end of most titles was cut off in results; truncated titles get lower click-through. | `_includes/head.html` now renders the `<title>` itself: optional `seo_title` front matter, else `title`; the brand is appended only when the whole fits in 60. 82 posts got a `seo_title` (≤ 60, keyword first); their on-page H1 is unchanged. Result: **0 of 181 pages over 60**, median 53, all unique. |
| 2 | **Risk series hub was `noindex` and excluded from the sitemap** (hard-coded front matter), although Risk posts have been live since early October. Tax hub the same. | The hub is the page that ties a topic cluster together; Google couldn't index it. | Removed the hard-coded flags. `head.html` now noindexes a series hub automatically only while it has no published post — Tax flips to indexable by itself when its first post goes live on 27 Oct. Both hubs are in the sitemap. |
| 3 | **Duplicate meta descriptions**: About, Privacy, Case study, the series index and all six series hubs shared the site-wide 209-character description; Methodology and Glossary were over 160. | Duplicate and over-length descriptions are reported in Search Console and Google rewrites them. | Unique 142–159-character descriptions on all 12 pages. |
| 4 | **Site name in structured data was "Himanshu Gupta"** (`WebSite` JSON-LD `name`, from `social.name` in `_config.yml`); the homepage title was 150+ characters. | Google uses the `WebSite` name for the site name shown in results. | `social.name: Wealth Primer`; homepage `seo_title: "Wealth Primer: financial education for Indian investors"`. Post authors now carry `url: /about/`. |
| 5 | **No breadcrumb structured data.** | Breadcrumbs replace the raw dated URL in results with *Home › Series › Mutual Funds, Minus the Marketing*. | `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD on every post (validated: all JSON-LD on all pages parses). |
| 6 | **53 chart images without width/height, none lazy-loaded** (largest SVG 384 KB). | Layout shift (CLS) and wasted bandwidth on mobile — Core Web Vitals signals. | `scripts/add_img_dims.py` adds dimensions from each SVG's viewBox (`--check` mode for CI); the post layout adds `loading="lazy" decoding="async"` to content images. |

Smaller: the 404 page had two `<h1>`s (now one). The write-post skill (`seo.md`,
`post-skeleton.md`, `lint_post.py`) now knows about `seo_title`, the 60-character rule,
breadcrumbs, the image script and the hub-noindex trap.

## Only the owner can do these

1. **Search Console: verify a *Domain* property** for `wealthprimer.in` (DNS TXT record).
   It covers http, https and www in one property. There is no verification meta tag in
   the site, so if a property exists it was verified another way — check it is a Domain
   property, not a URL-prefix one. Then:
   - submit `https://wealthprimer.in/sitemap.xml`;
   - use URL Inspection → *Request indexing* on `/`, `/series/`, `/series/risk/`
     (newly indexable) and the newest posts;
   - expect `/series/tax/` under "Excluded by noindex" until 27 Oct — that is by design.
2. **`www.wealthprimer.in` does not resolve.** Add a DNS `CNAME www → himanshug82.github.io`;
   GitHub Pages then redirects www to the apex. Today anyone typing www, and any backlink
   using it, gets an error.
3. **AdSense** (the `google-adsense-account` meta is present): if you apply, add an
   `ads.txt` at the site root with the line AdSense gives you, and update `/privacy/`
   to disclose ad cookies before ads go live.
4. **Optional:** the `BlogPosting` `publisher` is still the person (jekyll-seo-tag ties it
   to the author). Harmless; changing it needs a hand-written Organization block.

## Recommendations for the content (not done here)

- **Cite primary sources in the 41 posts with no external links.** For a YMYL site,
  links to SEBI circulars, AMFI data and company filings are trust signals; the newer
  posts already do this.
- **Sweep the owed internal links after 7 November** (listed in `TODO.md`): older posts
  that should link to the new mutual-funds posts once they are live.
- **Refresh, don't add, when Search Console shows page-2 queries.** Monthly: export
  queries at position 8–20, improve those posts, set `last_modified_at` only for
  substantive changes.
- **Leave the dated URLs alone.** `/2026/10/26/slug/` isn't ideal for evergreen
  explainers, but changing it would break every link with no redirect plugin installed.
- **7 posts under 600 words** are glossary entries and fine as they are; don't pad.
